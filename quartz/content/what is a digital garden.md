title: What is a digital garden
tags:

A digital garden is a place to collect and connect ideas as they grow — less polished than a blog, more alive than a folder of files.

Notes are interlinked, so reading one often leads you to another. Over time the garden becomes a little map of how I think.

If you're curious how this one is built and updated, see [[how this garden works]].
